Positioning

An independent audio framework, builder and marketplace built around using generative processes and systems to create audio tools that are scaleable and compatible by design and default, with the ability to drop in or use pre-existing engines. Build a plugin around your favourite sound, without being held hostage to the original source; while it would be great if we could own everything, there aren't enough resources and energy on planet earth for us all to own a studio's worth of equipment.

Visual idea

sOURnd invites you to imagine what you play with what you create. The brand character encourages experimentation for new things or reclaiming technology through expression. Understanding is enough to help you design.

Construction
sOURnd, with OUR capitalised. The O carries a knob pointer at 40°, the U a fader with its thumb in the upper third, the R a leg that ends in a jack. Every separation is a cut through the letter, so the mark works on any background. In running text the name is written “sournd”.

Rules. Light petrol is the one accent: buttons, links, selected states, focus rings, and the icon tile. Text on it is paper, as on raspberry; on amber it is ink. In dark mode the primary is #6BBCC6 and its label is ink. Raspberry is the secondary accent; chalk and paper are surfaces and text, not accents. Deep petrol appears only in the logo. Raspberry never fills a surface or marks status; it is the colour of the thing you touch. Amber is never a warning.
Contrast. Ink on paper 14.1:1. Paper on light petrol 3.0:1 and paper on raspberry 7.4:1 (button labels; the first is below AA for text, kept as a brand call, so buttons use 500 weight at 15px or larger). Ink on amber 7.8:1. Light petrol as text on paper 3.6:1, so links are ink with a light-petrol underline. Chalk on night 15.2:1. Dark primary #6BBCC6 on night 9.4:1, ink text on it 11.9:1. Raspberry as text on paper 7.4:1; on dark use #EE8FA6.
